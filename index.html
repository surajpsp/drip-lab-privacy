Privacy policy — at a glance

Drip Lab’s current Android app works offline. It does not require an account, contain advertising, or include analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. SilentDev does not collect or receive personal data automatically through gameplay.

The app stores game progress and preferences on your device. Android may back up that local data according to your device settings. Visiting this page or sending us an email involves the separate services described below.

This policy covers Drip Lab by SilentDev, Android package com.silentdev.driplab.

1. Data stored on your device

To make the game work, Drip Lab saves:

  • Completed levels and the stars you have earned, used to remember your progress and unlock labs.
  • Your choices for sound, music, haptics, reduced motion and liquid symbols.
  • Whether you have completed the introductory tutorial.

These values are stored in the app’s private local preferences. The app does not send them to SilentDev. The current Android app has no internet permission and does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or advertising identifier.

There are no in-app accounts, advertising networks, in-app purchases, social features or developer-operated cloud synchronization in the current version. SilentDev does not sell gameplay data or share it with advertisers.

2. Android backup and device transfer

Android backup is enabled for Drip Lab. Depending on your device, operating system and backup settings, Android or your device provider may back up and restore the app’s local preferences, including during a device transfer or reinstallation.

These backups are managed by your operating system or backup provider, not by SilentDev. We do not receive or have access to them. You can manage backup settings through your device or provider. Restoring a backup may restore previously saved progress.

Learn more about Android backup and restoration and Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. Information you choose to send us

If you email surajpsp@gmail.com, we receive your email address, any name supplied by your email service, and the message and attachments you choose to provide. Please send only what is needed to describe your question; do not include passwords or sensitive personal information.

We use this information to answer your request, troubleshoot issues and maintain relevant support correspondence. Our email provider, Google, processes messages to deliver and store email under its own privacy terms. We do not use support messages for advertising or sell them.

Where applicable, we may also retain or disclose relevant information when required by law or necessary to address misuse or establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

4. Visiting this privacy policy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub receives technical request information, which may include your IP address, browser details and access logs, to provide and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

SilentDev has not added analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, embedded media or cookies to this page. Links to external websites open services governed by their own privacy policies. Opening the privacy policy from the app uses your browser; gameplay data is not attached to the link.

5. How information is protected

Local preferences use Android’s app-private storage. The protection of operating-system backups depends on your device and backup provider. This page is served over HTTPS. We limit use of support messages to the purposes described above.

No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid including unnecessary personal data in support requests and protect access to your device and email account.

6. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local game data

Saved progress and preferences remain on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. To clear them on most Android devices, open Settings → Apps → Drip Lab → Storage → Clear storage. Menu names vary by device. This resets your progress and preferences.

Uninstalling or clearing storage may not remove a separate operating-system backup. Manage or delete backups through your device or backup provider if you do not want the data restored.

Support correspondence

We retain support messages only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request, maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us. We will respond in accordance with applicable law and explain any information we must retain.

There is no Drip Lab account to delete and no developer-hosted gameplay database. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights, including the right to object to or restrict processing, request portability, or complain to your local privacy regulator. Contact us to make a request.

7. Children’s privacy

The current game does not ask players for names, dates of birth, email addresses or other personal information, and does not automatically collect personal information through gameplay. The local-data and backup practices described above apply to all players.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, please contact us so we can review the message and delete it where appropriate. A parent or guardian can also clear the game’s local data using the steps above.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or our data practices change. The revised policy will appear at this address with a new “Last updated” date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before introducing new data practices.
